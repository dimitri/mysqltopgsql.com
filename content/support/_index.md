pgloader is open source, developed in public, and free to use — that's not
changing. But "open source" and "maintained for free forever" aren't the
same promise. If pgloader is part of how your production migration actually
happened, or part of how your CI pipeline loads test data every day, its
ongoing maintenance is worth funding the same way any other piece of your
infrastructure is.

Support for pgloader — and pg_auto_failover, pgcopydb, and pgextwlist — is
handled through [oss.theartofpostgresql.com](https://oss.theartofpostgresql.com/pgloader/):
one subscription, tiered by how much priority your production actually
needs.
